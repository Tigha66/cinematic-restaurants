Culinary Vision

Executive Chef
Thomas Allan

"Each dish is a collaboration between flavor and design, inspired by art, seasonality, and the pursuit of perfection."

Under Chef Allan's direction, The Modern's ever-evolving menus showcase the finest seasonal ingredients through innovative techniques rooted in classical training. His approach honors tradition while embracing contemporary creativity—a philosophy that has earned the restaurant its two Michelin stars and four James Beard Awards.
