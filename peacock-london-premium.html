Our Philosophy

Respect for ingredients. Restraint in preparation.

At Peacock London, we believe the finest ingredients need little embellishment. Sourced from trusted fishmongers, artisan farms, and seasonal suppliers, each component is treated with care and intention.

"We don't create dishes to showcase technique. We create technique to showcase the dish."

This philosophy guides every plate, every pairing, and every moment of your evening with us.

A Commitment to Excellence

Since opening, Peacock London has been guided by a single principle: let the ingredients lead. Inspired by Le Bernardin's reverence for seafood and Eleven Madison Park's focus on seasonality, we've built a restaurant that honors provenance, craft, and connection.

Our team works with trusted suppliers—fishmongers who bring in the day's catch at dawn, farmers who grow rare heirloom vegetables, and artisans who cure, age, and ferment with precision.

"The fish is the star of the plate. Everything else is there to support it."

Join us for an experience where culinary artistry meets iconic Thames views.
