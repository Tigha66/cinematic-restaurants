Our Story

Where every morning begins with warmth

At Fresh Baked Café, we believe in the simple magic of scratch-made baking. Every croissant is laminated by hand. Every loaf proves overnight. Every pastry emerges golden from our ovens before dawn. This is the Midwest way — honest, comforting, made with care.

A Day at the Bakery

From Dawn to Noon

4:00 AM — Dawn

The Oven

Proof boxes humming, the first golden loaves emerge. The bakery fills with the scent of fresh bread.

7:00 AM — Morning

The Counter

Glass cases filled with possibility. Croissants, scones, and pastries that catch the morning light.

12:00 PM — Noon

The Gathering

Lunch crowds, sandwich boards, and the hum of community. Tables filled with neighbors and friends.
