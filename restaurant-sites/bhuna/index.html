Our Philosophy

Spice, craft, and Pacific Northwest soul

At Bhuna, we honor the rich traditions of Indian cuisine while embracing the bounty of the Pacific Northwest. Every dish tells a story — of ancestral recipes passed down through generations, reimagined with locally-sourced ingredients from Oregon's farms and waters.

Signature Dishes

From Our Kitchen

01

Bhuna Lamb

Slow-cooked lamb shoulder with caramelized onions, tomatoes, and our signature bhuna spice blend. Served with saffron rice.

02

Pacific Salmon Tikka

Wild-caught Oregon salmon marinated in tandoor spices, grilled over open flame. Northwest fish meets ancestral technique.

03

Craft Chai Flight

Three preparations of our house chai — traditional masala, lavender honey, and cold-brewed cardamom. A ritual in every cup.

04

Tandoori Vegetables

Seasonal vegetables from local farms, marinated in yogurt and spices, charred in our clay tandoor oven.

05

Duck Vindaloo

Willamette Valley duck in fiery Goan vindaloo sauce. Bold, tangy, unforgettable. For those who embrace the heat.

06

Cardamom Kulfi

Traditional Indian ice cream infused with green cardamom and Oregon hazelnuts. A sweet ending to your journey.
